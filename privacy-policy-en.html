Niyaz Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Niyaz is a spiritual journal application provided by Niyaz Mobile that processes data primarily on the user’s device. The application has no user account system, Niyaz-operated backend, or cloud synchronization.

Data stored locally

Prayer tracking, Quran goals, reading position, bookmarks, personal prayers, dhikr counters, theme selection, and notification preferences are stored on the device. The application is configured to exclude its data from Android operating system cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Location and notifications

Approximate location may be used on the device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. This information is not sent to a server operated by Niyaz. Users may instead enter a city or coordinates manually.

Notification permission is optional. If exact alarm access is unavailable, the application uses inexact scheduling that may be delayed by the operating system.

External applications and services

The user chooses the destination application when sharing content. In the “Nearby Mosques” feature, after explicit confirmation, a search query and optional approximate coordinates may be sent to a map application or Google Maps web page. Those external services are governed by their own privacy terms.

Content sources

Arabic Quran text is sourced from Tanzil Project, Turkish translation from QuranEnc, and approved hadith content from HadeethEnc. Content versions and attribution information are available in the application’s “Content Sources” screen.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

This release contains no advertising, AdMob, analytics, crash reporting, or user-tracking SDK. Religious usage activity is not sent by Niyaz to an external user profile.

Deleting data

The Settings > Reset data option deletes user preferences, SQLite records, and pending Niyaz notifications. Uninstalling the application also removes local application data from the device.

Product limitations

Niyaz does not provide medical, legal, professional advisory, or religious ruling services. The application’s store target audience is age 13 and over.
