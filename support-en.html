Niyaz Support

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Developer: Niyaz Mobile

Support email: niyaz.app.support@gmail.com

Reporting a problem

Include the device model, Android version, Niyaz application version, and steps required to reproduce the issue. Do not send personal prayer text, precise location coordinates, keystore files, passwords, or other sensitive information.

Deleting data

Use Settings > Reset data to remove local user records and pending Niyaz notifications. Niyaz does not maintain user accounts, so a separate account deletion request is not required.

Permissions

Location and notification permissions can be changed in Android system settings. Manual location selection and core offline content remain available without these permissions.

Response time

Support requests are reviewed as soon as reasonably possible. Response times may vary depending on the scope of the request.
